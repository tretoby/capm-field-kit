Hall B Row 14 high-density capacity

BC-HB14-001 · v1.2Jul 13, 2026

Problem / opportunity

Internal AI workloads need 24 GPU racks of new capacity by Q4. Hall B has floor space and power headroom in Row 14, but no high-density cooling or power distribution.

Options analyzed

OptionCostReady inOutcome
1. Do nothing$0n/aCapacity shortfall; workloads delayed
2. Lease colocation space$1.1M/yr6 wksFast but recurring cost, less control
3. Build out Row 14 in-house$480K once8 wksRecommended

Cost-benefit

Option 3 pays back in about 5 months against Option 2's lease cost. NPV over 3 years at 8% favors Option 3 by roughly $2.3M.

Success measures

  • 24 racks live and accepted by Oct 30, 2026
  • Rack inlet temp held at or under 27 °C at full load
  • Within ±5% of the $480K budget

HB14 GPU Rack Deployment

PRJ-HB14-SS-001 · v1.0Baselined Aug 14, 2026

Product scope description

24 GPU racks in Hall B, Row 14, each with dual-fed PDUs, an RDHx cooling door, and four 400G uplinks, burned in and accepted for production.

Deliverables

  • Approved row layout and electrical one-line drawing
  • Installed and labeled racks, power, cooling and cabling
  • Burn-in test reports for all 24 racks
  • As-built documentation and handoff package

Acceptance criteria

  • 48-hour burn-in at 90% load with no thermal alarms
  • Every link passes optical loss test (≤ 2.5 dB)
  • Labeling matches the as-built drawings

Exclusions

  • Server OS imaging and configuration
  • Workload migration
  • Changes to Rows 1–13

Constraints

  • Electrical work only in approved Sunday maintenance windows
  • Fixed budget of $480K

Assumptions

  • Floor rated for 1,500 kg per rack position (to be verified)
  • Customer supplies the servers by Sep 25

Risk register & probability-impact matrix

ECO domainFundamentals & Core Concepts
WhenPlanning, reviewed throughout
OwnerPM; each risk has a risk owner
Try it. Change probability (P) or impact (I) and the score (P × I) and heat map update. Threat responses: avoid, mitigate, transfer, escalate, accept. Opportunity responses: exploit, enhance, share, escalate, accept.

Rows are probability (5 = very likely), columns are impact. Scores of 12+ are high priority, 5–11 medium, 1–4 low.

Earned value management

ECO domainPredictive, Plan-Based
WhenMonitoring & controlling
OwnerProject manager
Try it. Pick a status week, then set how much work is actually done and what it actually cost. CPI and SPI below 1.0 are bad. Memory hook: EV comes first in every formula (CV = EV − AC, SV = EV − PV, CPI = EV ÷ AC, SPI = EV ÷ PV).

BAC = $480,000. Planned cumulative spend by week: 8%, 20%, 35%, 52%, 70%, 88%, 100%. EAC here uses BAC ÷ CPI (current cost performance continues). TCPI = (BAC − EV) ÷ (BAC − AC).
